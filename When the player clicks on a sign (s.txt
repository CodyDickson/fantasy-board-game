When the player clicks on a sign (such as to exit camp), they get the confirmation window.

A player must Travel before they can interact with the world.

Once the player has moved, the interaction range is shown on the board.

Clicking on an empty tile within range opens the Info GUI and gives the player the option to build a village.

Clicking on a village within range opens the Info GUI and provides some general stats about the village.
