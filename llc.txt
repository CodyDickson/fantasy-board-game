Combat Brainstorming
-
Board combat:
-If a monster has the modifier "Hostile", they will deal damage to the player when the player moves past. The player will retaliate automatically and continue moving.
-When a player lands on a monster, their combat score is removed from the monster's health. The monster retaliates, dealing their combat score damage to the player.
-The player can select "Attack" and choose a target within range, including the tile they are on. If the monster is within range for their attack they will retaliate.
-Fighters have two Equipment slots and armor.
-Wizards have one Equipment slot and their weapons are high range, sometimes affecting multiple tiles.
