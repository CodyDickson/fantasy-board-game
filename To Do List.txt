Classes:
All classes begin with a unique weapon and have special modifiers. Classes are unlocked by completing Achievements.
[ ] Nomad: Unlocked from the start. Weapon is a walking stick (1-3 damage). Landing on an unowned tile permanently claims it.
[ ] Fighter: Starts with a Sword (3-5 damage). Chance to claim a tile after winning combat.
[ ] Wizard: Starts with a Magic Staff (3 damage, long ranged). Spend available action points to claim nearby tiles.
[ ] Gambler: Starts with a Bag of Dice (roll 1-6 when attacked, even numbers survive). Every five turns, can bet on which player will have the highest score in five turns.
[ ] Barbarian: Has two equipment slots. Starts with two Axes (2-3 Damage).
[ ] Cultist: Starts with a Dagger (1-3 Damage). On game start, choose one enemy type - each time they are beaten in combat, claim tiles.
[ ] Hunter: Starts with a Bow (2-3 damage, long ranged). Enemies will become randomly marked. Killing them claims local tiles.
[ ] Lich: Starts with a Tentacle (1-7 damage, long ranged). Defeated enemies have a chance to rise again as servants of the Lich.
[ ] Cat: Starts with Claws (1-3 damage, chance to hit twice). Begins with nine lives.
[ ] Cleric: Starts with a Mace (2-4 damage). Can fully heal each turn for 1 AP.
[ ] Peddler: Starts without a weapon. Can build villages for half the cost. Earns extra gold in all scenarios.
[ ] Diviner: Starts with a Candle (1-3 damage, inflicts burn). Able to travel randomly across the board.
[ ] Architect: Has two equipment slots. Starts with a Greathammer (4-8 damage, two-handed). Builds dungeons instead of villages.
[ ] Void: Must first claim Camp then spreads one tile at a time. Only has one life.

BRAINSTORMING
Players have 3 Actions. Travel costs 1. Resting (recover health) costs 1. Raiding costs 1. Fighting another player costs 1. Building a village costs 1.

Player turn begins: MAGENTA FIGHTER, WHITE WIZARD, etc.
3 Actions.
Move button is enabled. Villages are enabled and the player can click on their own villages to upgrade them by spending gold.
Clicking move rolls the dice and shows the arrow keys (if more than one direction are available). Costs 1 Action to move.
While moving, a "passing encounter" can occur:
-aggressive monster (monster damages player, player returns attack on the monster)
-aggressive player (opposing player first, then traveling player)
-crossroads (choose a direction)
-pay toll (to continue forward)
-merchants (to shop)
When landing, a "landing encounter" can occur:
-fight monster
-earn gold from owned village
-pay gold to opposing village
-raid dungeon
-merchant
Player turn ends. Player values are updated to match current player values.
