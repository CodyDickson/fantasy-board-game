BRAINSTORMING
Players have 3 Actions. Travel costs 1. Resting (recover health) costs 1. Raiding costs 1. Fighting another player costs 1. Building a village costs 1.

Prisms located around the map. Each time one spawns, the first player to reach it chooses a category - Gold, Combat. Whichever one gives that player a bonus in that type. Towards the end of the game when a certain percentage of the map has been opened up, multiple Prisms spawn. Whichever one got more 'votes' (Gold, Combat), that becomes the winning factor for the game board. Combat means all other players must be eliminated. Gold gives a gold number that must be hit to trigger victory (or countdown of 10 turns).

Player turn begins: MAGENTA FIGHTER, WHITE WIZARD, etc.
3 Actions.
Move button is enabled. Villages are enabled and the player can click on their own villages to upgrade them by spending gold.
Clicking move rolls the dice and shows the arrow keys (if more than one direction are available). Costs 1 Action to move.
While moving, a "passing encounter" can occur:
-aggressive monster (monster damages player, player returns attack on the monster)
-aggressive player (opposing player first, then traveling player)
-crossroads (choose a direction)
-pay toll (to continue forward)
-merchants (to shop)
When landing, a "landing encounter" can occur:
-fight monster
-earn gold from owned village
-pay gold to opposing village
-raid dungeon
-merchant
Player turn ends. Player values are updated to match current player values.
