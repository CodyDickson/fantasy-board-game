BRAINSTORMING
Players have 1 Weapon Slot, 3 Oddity Slots, and 2 Consumable Slots. Each class starts with a unique weapon but they can change their weapon if they find one on the board. Artifacts can be swapped out and provide bonuses.

Player turn begins. Move button is enabled. Villages are enabled and the player can click on their own villages to upgrade them.
While moving, a "passing encounter" can occur:
-aggressive monster
-aggressive player
-crossroads
-pay toll
When landing, a "landing encounter" can occur:
-fight monster
-earn gold from owned village
-pay gold to opposing village
-raid dungeon
-merchant
Player turn ends. Player values are updated to match current player values.
